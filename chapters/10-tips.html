Tips for Identifying a Contractor

When taking steps to harden your home, it is important to identify a trustworthy contractor who will do the best job possible to protect your home. There are several key requirements that a contractor must meet to be trustworthy, such as obtaining a state license and having reputable references from past clients. You must also be mindful of common construction scams in order to ensure that the correct work is completed and you do not overpay.
